# NimbusWare Returns & Exchanges for Physical Goods

## Scope
This policy covers physical NimbusWare merchandise: onboarding kits, individual hardware security keys, and printed guide bundles. Software subscriptions are governed separately by the Refund Policy.

## 30-Day Return Window
Physical goods may be returned within 30 calendar days of the recorded delivery date. Start a return from Settings > Billing > Orders, or reply to your shipping confirmation email stating the reason for the return. Returns started on day 31 or later are declined.

## Condition Requirements
Items must be undamaged and complete. A full kit return must include both security keys, all printed guides, the laminated checklist, and any included cables. Missing components reduce the refund by the replacement cost of the missing item — a single security key is valued at $29.00. Original packaging is preferred but not mandatory.

## Defective Unit Exchanges
For defective units we cross-ship: the replacement ships before the old unit comes back, so you are never left without hardware. Report the defect through a support ticket describing the failure and attaching photos if the defect is visible. Once the defect is confirmed — usually within 1 business day — the replacement dispatches within 2 business days together with a free prepaid return label for the faulty unit. The old unit must ship back within 14 days of the replacement's delivery; unreturned defective units are charged to the card on file.

## Refund Timing and Method
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 business days of the returned item arriving at our warehouse. Inspections are completed within 2 business days of arrival, and you receive an email confirming the inspection result and the exact refund amount.

## Return Shipping Costs and Restocking Fee
Return shipping is free for defective items: a prepaid label is issued through the support ticket and emailed as a PDF. Non-defect returns — changed mind, duplicate order, wrong kit count — use the same prepaid label, but a $6 restocking fee is deducted from the refund. The restocking fee does not apply to defective units or to shipments we got wrong.

## Items That Cannot Be Returned
Security keys activated as live credentials cannot be returned for security reasons unless defective; activation permanently binds a key's firmware attestation to the purchasing workspace. Sealed, unactivated keys follow the standard 30-day window normally.
