# NimbusWare Billing FAQ

## Where Can I Find My Invoices?
Every invoice appears under Settings > Billing > Invoices. Each entry can be downloaded as a PDF or exported to CSV for accounting tools. Invoices are generated on the same calendar day each month as your original signup and emailed automatically to the designated billing contact. Owners and billing admins can download 36 months of history directly; older documents are available on request.

## How Does Per-Seat Proration Work?
Seats added mid-cycle are billed immediately for the remaining days in the current cycle. On a Team plan costing $15.00 per seat per month, adding a seat with 20 of 30 days remaining produces an immediate charge of $10.00. Removed seats take effect right away; their unused value is not cash-refunded but credited against your next invoice at the same daily rate. Adding and removing seats within one billing period nets into a single adjustment line on that invoice.

## What Happens If a Payment Fails?
Failed payments trigger 3 automatic retries spread over 10 days: on day 3, day 7, and day 10 after the original attempt, with a reminder email after each retry. If all three retries fail, the workspace pauses into a read-only state on day 10 — projects stay visible but editing, exporting, and integrations stop. Paused workspaces keep data for 90 days before deletion. Updating the payment method during the retry window triggers an immediate re-charge.

## How Do I Update My Payment Method?
Go to Settings > Billing > Payment Methods. Only workspace owners and billing admins can make changes. Up to 3 cards can be stored with one marked as default; card details are handled by our payment processor and never stored on NimbusWare servers. Changes apply starting with the next invoice.

## How Are Sales Tax and VAT Handled?
Listed prices exclude tax. Applicable sales tax, GST, or VAT is added at checkout based on billing country and postal code. Businesses in the EU, UK, Australia, and similar jurisdictions can enter a valid VAT/GST ID to be exempted under reverse charge. Registered tax IDs appear on every invoice.

## When Does a Downgrade Take Effect?
Plan downgrades always take effect at the end of the current billing cycle, so you keep features already paid for; the change shows as Scheduled until renewal. Upgrades apply immediately instead, with a prorated charge for the rest of the cycle. A scheduled downgrade can be cancelled any time before renewal.
