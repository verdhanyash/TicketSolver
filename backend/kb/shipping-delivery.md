# NimbusWare Shipping & Delivery Policy

## About the Onboarding Kit
The NimbusWare onboarding kit is an optional physical product containing two hardware USB security keys, printed administrator and end-user guides, and a laminated rollout checklist. Kits are ordered from Settings > Security > Hardware Keys and are entirely optional; every software feature works without one.

## Shipping Options and Timelines
Two options are offered at checkout. Standard shipping arrives in 5 to 7 business days and costs a flat $8.00. Express shipping arrives in 2 business days and costs $25.00. Orders placed before 2:00 PM Eastern Time on a business day leave our Ohio warehouse the same day; later orders ship the next business day. Estimates exclude carrier delays such as weather or customs holds.

## Tracking Numbers
A tracking number is emailed to the address on the order as soon as the kit is dispatched. Allow up to 24 hours for the carrier's first scan to appear online. If no tracking email arrives within 2 business days of ordering, contact support with your order number.

## Lost Packages
If tracking has not updated for 7 consecutive days or the carrier marks the parcel lost, wait until 10 calendar days have passed beyond the expected delivery date before contacting us. At that point NimbusWare ships a free replacement kit at the same shipping speed as the original order and files the carrier claim ourselves. Packages marked delivered but missing follow the same rule: report them after the 10-day window past expected delivery, since carriers often post late scans.

## Damaged on Arrival
Kits that arrive damaged are replaced free of charge. Report damage within 7 days of delivery through a support ticket that includes your order number and at least 2 photos of the damage. We dispatch the replacement immediately; you do not need to return the damaged unit first, though we may request it later with a prepaid label.

## Where Kits Ship
Onboarding kits ship only to countries matching the workspace's billing-address country. Workspaces billed in a country outside our shipping network see hardware ordering disabled in Settings and can request digital PDF copies of the printed guides instead. Kits cannot ship to post office boxes or freight forwarders.
