# NimbusWare Refund Policy

## Overview
This policy covers all NimbusWare subscription plans (Starter, Team, Business, Enterprise) and applies to every charge on an account, including renewals and seat additions. All amounts are calculated and paid in USD.

## Full Refund Window: First 14 Days
Any subscription payment may be refunded in full if the request is submitted within 14 days of the charge date. This applies to first-time purchases, renewals, and upgrades alike. No explanation is required and no processing fee is deducted.

## Partial Prorated Refunds: Days 15 to 30
Requests submitted between day 15 and day 30 after the charge date receive a partial refund covering the unused portion of the term, calculated on a daily basis. For example, a $365.00 annual plan cancelled on day 20 refunds the remaining 345 days, approximately $345.00. Monthly plans are prorated against their 30-day cycle the same way.

## No Refunds After 30 Days
After 30 days from the charge date, subscriptions are non-refundable. The single exception is service outage credit: if NimbusWare's measured uptime falls below 99.9% in a calendar month, affected workspaces automatically receive an account credit worth 10% of that month's fee for every full percentage point below 99.9%, capped at 100% of one month's fee. Credits apply to future invoices and expire 12 months after issue.

## Supervisor Approval Requirement
Refund requests totalling more than $50.00 USD require approval from a human support supervisor before they can be processed. Agents may approve and release refunds of $50.00 USD or less on their own. Requests above the threshold add up to 2 business days of review time. The threshold applies to the total amount per refund request, not per line item.

## Duplicate Charges
Confirmed duplicate charges are refunded within 5 business days of verification. Duplicate-charge refunds above $50.00 USD still pass through supervisor sign-off before funds are released, but the 5-business-day clock starts when the duplicate is confirmed, not when approval finishes.

## Annual Plans Cancelled Mid-Cycle
When an annual plan is cancelled partway through its term outside the 14-day window, the unused value is credited to the NimbusWare account balance rather than returned to the card. The balance is applied automatically to future invoices and seat upgrades, remains valid while the workspace stays active, and cannot be withdrawn as cash.

## How to Request a Refund
Workspace owners and billing admins submit requests from Settings > Billing > Request Refund, or by emailing billing@nimbusware.com from the registered address. Once approved, card refunds settle in 5 to 10 business days depending on the issuing bank.
